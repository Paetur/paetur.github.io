iOS support ≠ every software feature

iOS 27 is the latest publicly released major generation. Apple lists iPhone 11, SE (2nd generation) and later as compatible. For these phones, “27 · current” means the highest released compatible generation today; Apple has not announced their final lifetime iOS version. Legacy values are final major generations, not patch versions or security-support promises. Apple skipped the public iOS 19–25 names.

The AI check means Apple Intelligence hardware eligibility: 15 Pro / Pro Max and all 16-family and later models. It does not promise every AI feature. Some iOS 27 AI functions require a newer on-device model, including Air, 17 Pro and later Pro models, and Duo.

European SIMs & Faroese carriers

The eSIM column describes hardware in European variants. XS / XR onward and SE (2nd / 3rd generation) support eSIM. Most Irish variants also have nano-SIM; iPhone Air and Duo are eSIM-only. Apple’s Irish 18 Pro specs list nano-SIM + eSIM, unlike the US specification. US 14-series and later models are eSIM-only; newer generations have additional eSIM-only markets. China mainland, Hong Kong and Macao variants can differ: check the exact model before importing.

Føroya Tele and Nema both officially offer smartphone eSIM. Activation, plan eligibility, Quick Transfer, roaming and 5G depend on the carrier; an eSIM check does not guarantee these services. FT’s published device examples are not a current certification list for every 2026 model.

Satellite, AI regions & USB speeds

Satellite SOS hardware starts with the iPhone 14 family and is included on 16e, 17e, Air and Duo; it is absent from SE (3rd generation). The Faroe Islands are not listed in Apple’s verified Emergency SOS via satellite availability list. Support in a nearby country does not establish Faroese availability, and northern latitude or terrain can further limit reception.

Apple Intelligence needs supported language, region and software settings. Siri AI is currently English-only in the stable support documentation and unavailable on iOS in the EU; mainland China restrictions also apply. Faroese language support is not established. Faroese availability must not be inferred from Danish or EU availability.

USB-C is a connector, not a speed guarantee. Base, Plus, e-series and Air models with USB-C use USB 2 (up to 480Mb/s). 15 Pro and later Pro models, plus Duo, support USB 3 up to 10Gb/s with a suitable cable. Lightning iPhones use USB 2. MagSafe indicates built-in magnets and charging support; Qi alone does not imply MagSafe.

How to read hardware details

All listed displays without ProMotion are conventional 60Hz displays; Apple’s legacy spec sheets do not always explicitly state refresh rate. “2× sensor crop” and “8× optical-quality crop” are sensor-based modes, not additional physical telephoto lenses. “No telephoto” still allows digital zoom. LiDAR, Dynamic Island, buttons and modem names are called out where applicable; their absence from older notes does not imply support. N1 is a Wi-Fi/Bluetooth chip, not a cellular modem. Battery hours, where shown, are Apple’s “up to” local video playback figures, not real-world forecasts; test conditions vary between generations. No unverified mAh capacities are used.
